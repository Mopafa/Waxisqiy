**KR Puram Local Bulletin: September 1, 2026**

### **1. Traffic & Transit Updates**
*   **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge:** Heavy congestion reported during morning and evening rush hours. Commuters are advised to account for an additional **15–20 minutes** to cross the junction. The newly installed automated signals at the bridge entrance are currently operational to regulate flow from Mahadevapura and Kolar.
*   **Flyover Restriction Alert:** Starting **September 4, 2026**, the Bengaluru Traffic Police (BTP) will implement new restrictions on five major flyovers. While KR Puram’s bridge isn’t on the immediate "ban" list, nearby corridors like **Ballari Road** and the **Silk Board-Ragigudda** flyover will see 24-hour bans for two-wheelers and heavy vehicles.
*   **Metro Update:** Trials for the **Blue Line Phase 2A** (Silk Board to KR Puram) are scheduled to begin in October. Residents should watch for construction-related lane narrowings near the KR Puram station area.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Power Disconnection Notice:** Effective **today (Sept 1)**, BESCOM has begun a strict policy of immediate disconnection for consumers with electricity bills overdue by more than 30 days. Linemen are accompanying meter readers to cut connections for defaults exceeding ₹100.
*   **Monsoon Safety:** With ongoing rain, the **BBMP** has issued a high-risk alert for waterlogging at the KR Puram underpass. Residents are advised to avoid these low-lying areas during heavy downpours.
*   **Water Supply:** No specific supply disruptions are reported for KR Puram today. However, a major 60-hour city-wide water maintenance shutdown is planned for **September 15–17, 2026**.

### **3. Local News Highlights**
*   **Police Alert:** An investigation is underway following a stabbing incident in the KR Puram area earlier today involving a 21-year-old man. Increased police patrolling has been noted near the main market area.
*   **Infrastructure:** The state government has invited bids for a new **toll-based elevated corridor** linking KR Puram to Yeshwanthpur to further ease the Old Madras Road bottleneck.

### **4. Festival Clarification: Holi 2026**
*   **Holi 2026 has already passed.** It was celebrated on **March 3 and 4, 2026**. There are no Holi-related celebrations happening today. 
*   **Current Festivals:** Today marks **Raksha Panchami** (primarily observed in Odisha). Looking ahead, **Krishna Janmashtami** is scheduled for September 4, and **Ganesh Chaturthi** will begin on September 14, 2026.
