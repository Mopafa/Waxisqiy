**KR Puram News Bulletin | Sunday, October 25, 2026**

### **1. Traffic & Transit: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Old Madras Road Congestion:** Commuters are reporting heavy traffic on feeder roads including **Suranjan Das Road, 80 Feet Road, and 100 Feet Road**. Expect to wait through 2-3 signal cycles at the NGEF Junction and surrounding intersections.
*   **Tin Factory Bottleneck:** Standard congestion continues near the Tin Factory bus stop and KR Puram Railway Station due to bus idling and pedestrian crossings. Transit times for the 1.5 km stretch may exceed 30–40 minutes during peak hours.
*   **Hanging Bridge Condition:** Drivers are advised to use caution on the **KR Puram Cable-Stayed (Hanging) Bridge** due to reports of uneven surfaces and vegetation growth in structural cracks. No emergency closures are active today, but surface repairs are pending.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Metro Pink Line Update:** Official safety approval for the **KR Puram–Central Silk Board** elevated stretch is expected within the next 5 days. Trial runs and final inspections are nearing completion.
*   **Public Holiday Tomorrow:** While today is a regular Sunday, tomorrow (**Monday, October 26**) is a regional public holiday for **Maharishi Valmiki Jayanti**. Expect government offices, schools, and some banks to remain closed.
*   **Infrastructure:** Acceleration of the Metro Blue Line corridor (connecting KR Puram to Hebbal and the Airport) is ongoing; expect night-time construction activity and minor lane diversions near the median.

### **3. Festival & Events: Sharad Purnima**

*   **Local Cultural Events:**
