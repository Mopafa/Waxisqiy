**KR Puram Local News Bulletin: Monday, September 7, 2026**

### **Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Flyover Restrictions:** As of **September 4, 2026**, the Bengaluru Traffic Police have implemented new vehicle restrictions on five major flyovers. While the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge** is currently **exempt** from the 24/7 two-wheeler ban (which applies to Airport Road, Electronic City, and BGS flyovers), it remains under high surveillance.
*   **No Parking Zone:** A strict "No Parking" rule is in effect from **Benniganahalli to the Mahadevapura flyover** (including the Hanging Bridge stretch) to mitigate severe bottlenecks. Violators face immediate towing.
*   **Structural Watch:** Authorities have flagged visible vegetation and structural cracks on the Hanging Bridge. Expect periodic "slow-moving traffic" as minor maintenance and structural health monitoring (SHM) assessments are ongoing.
*   **Current Status:** Tin Factory remains a high-congestion zone. Commuters are advised to use the Purple Line Metro for travel towards Indiranagar or Whitefield to avoid the 20–30 minute delays common at this junction during peak hours.

### **Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Voter Revision (SIR 2026):** The Special Intensive Revision of the Electoral Roll is active today. **86 hearing centers** are open across the KR Puram Assembly Constituency for residents to verify voter details or complete enrollments.
*   **Security Crackdown:** The Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) and local police conducted a coordinated operation in KR Puram today, intercepting five foreign nationals for suspected illegal stay and drug-related activities. Residents are reminded to submit "Form-C" to the police within 24 hours when renting to foreign nationals.
*   **Power & Water:** There are no major scheduled BESCOM power shutdowns reported specifically for KR Puram today. However, residents are advised to check the **BESCOM Mithra app** as the main maintenance portal is undergoing technical updates.
*   **Weather:** The IMD has issued a yellow alert for light to moderate evening showers in East Bengaluru.

### **Holi 2026 & Local Festivals**
*   **Holi Correction:** **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 4, 2026.** There are no Holi celebrations scheduled for today.

*   **Upcoming Festival:** The 10-day **Ganesha Chaturthi** festival begins next Monday, **September 14, 2026**. BBMP has set up single-window clearance centers at the KR Puram ward office for pandal permissions starting today.
