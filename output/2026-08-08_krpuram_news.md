### **KR Puram Local News Bulletin: August 8, 2026**

#### **1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Current Status:** Expect standard heavy congestion at the **Tin Factory junction** and the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge**. Commuters are reporting 15–20 minute delays during peak morning hours.
*   **Bottlenecks:** Criss-crossing traffic between the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and Old Madras Road (OMR) remains the primary cause of slow movement. The automated signals at the bridge mouth are active to regulate flow toward Indiranagar and Hebbal.
*   **Pro-tip:** Residents traveling toward ITPL or Whitefield are advised to use the cable-stayed bridge to avoid the bottleneck under the railway bridge, as the Lowry Road Underbridge area continues to face minor tailbacks from recent maintenance.
