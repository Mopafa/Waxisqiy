**KR PURAM LOCAL NEWS BULLETIN: JULY 31, 2026**

### 🚨 **CIVIC ALERT: 15-Hour Water Supply Disruption**
The **Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB)** has announced a major 15-hour water cutoff for today, Friday, July 31, affecting a large portion of East and North Bengaluru.
*   **Time:** 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM
*   **Affected Localities (KR Puram & Vicinity):** Kalkere, Horamavu Agara, K. Channasandra, Varanasi, Benniganahalli, Belathur, and surrounding areas.
*   **Reason:** Emergency repair work to fix a leak in the scour valve of the 3,000 mm Cauvery Stage V transmission pipeline.
*   **Action Required:** Residents are advised to use stored water judiciously as supply may take until late tonight or early tomorrow morning to normalize.

### 🚦 **TRAFFIC WATCH: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
Congestion remains high across the KR Puram corridor due to ongoing infrastructure projects.
*   **Tin Factory Junction:** Heavy slow-moving traffic is reported. Avoid this stretch during peak evening hours (5:00 PM – 8:30 PM) if possible.
*   **Hanging Bridge (Cable-Stayed Bridge):** General congestion continues. Use the bridge as an alternative to the KR Puram railway station underpass, which often faces bottlenecks.
*   **Hebbal Flyover Impact:** Long-term construction of additional lanes at Hebbal continues to restrict the KR Puram up-ramp for heavy vehicles; expect ripple effects on traffic coming from the city toward KR Puram.
