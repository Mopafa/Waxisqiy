**KR Puram Resident Bulletin: Thursday, August 13, 2026**

### **1. Traffic & Bandh Alert (Tin Factory / Hanging Bridge)**
*   **Statewide Bandh:** A 12-hour "Karnataka Bandh" (6:00 AM – 6:00 PM) was called for today over the Cauvery water dispute.
*   **Current Status:** The impact is reported as **mixed to lukewarm**. Major groups (KRV, Hotel Association, and many School Unions) have withdrawn active support. Leading activist Vatal Nagaraj was reportedly detained early this morning.
*   **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge:** While traffic is currently flowing, commuters should expect **intermittent slow-moving traffic** and a higher police presence. Public transport (BMTC/KSRTC) is operational but may run fewer services if protests intensify near the ORR-Old Madras Road junction.
*   **Airport Advisory:** If traveling to KIA via the KR Puram route, allow an extra 45–60 minutes as highway blockages have been reported at other city exit points.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts (Power & Water)**
*   **BESCOM Power Outage:** Scheduled maintenance is affecting nearby zones today. While KR Puram’s main commercial hub is stable, parts of **Hoodi, Varthur pockets, and Horamavu** (East Zone) are facing outages scheduled from **10:00 AM to 5:00 PM**. Check the *BESCOM Mithra* app for your specific feeder status.
*   **Water Supply:** BWSSB has confirmed that Cauvery water supply remains stable for the city today. There are no emergency shutdowns scheduled for the Mahadevapura zone; however, localized stress is reported in areas dependent on borewells due to groundwater depletion.
*   **Health Alert:** The Food Safety and Quality Department is conducting active raids today across Bengaluru following a major seizure of 132kg of "rotten food" in the city earlier this week. Residents are advised to be cautious when dining at local street stalls.

### **3. Holi 2026 Clarification**
*   **Correction:** There are **no Holi celebrations occurring today**. Holi 2026 was celebrated earlier this year on **Wednesday, March 4, 2026**. 
*   Today is a regular working Thursday (notwithstanding the bandh call). The next major public holiday in Karnataka is Independence Day this coming Saturday.

### **Summary for Residents:**
*   **Commute:** Tin Factory is open, but stay alert for sudden flash protests.
*   **Power:** Keep devices charged if you are in the Horamavu/Hoodi fringe areas.
*   **Safety:** Stick to reputable food outlets due to ongoing safety raids.
