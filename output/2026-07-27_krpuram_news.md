# Local News Bulletin: KR Puram, Bengaluru
**Date:** Monday, July 27, 2026

### 🚦 Traffic Report: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge
*   **Current Status:** Heavy congestion is reported at the **Tin Factory Junction** and the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge**. Commuters should expect slow-moving traffic (gridlock) at the base of the flyover on the Byapanahalli side in both directions.
*   **Key Bottlenecks:** The automated signal at the mouth of the Hanging Bridge is causing delays of **15–20 minutes** during peak hours. Additional bottlenecks are reported near the KR Puram Railway Station due to bus stops occupying multiple lanes.
*   **Ongoing Impact:** Metro Blue Line construction (KR Puram–Silk Board stretch) continues to restrict road width, contributing to the daily pile-up. 
*   **Recommendation:** Use the "Old Madras Road" service lanes cautiously; expect significant delays for those traveling toward Indiranagar or Hebbal.

### 🏛️ Civic & Infrastructure Alerts
*   **Metro Update:** Greater Bengaluru Development Minister Krishna Byre Gowda has directed BMRCL to begin **trial runs for the Blue Line** in October 2026, with commercial operations slated for 2027.
*   **ORR Redevelopment:** A ₹450 crore project for the redevelopment of the Outer Ring Road (ORR) stretch is underway. Residents are encouraged to provide suggestions on the draft design currently being finalized by consultants.
*   **Local Security:** Investigation continues into the high-profile Seegehalli triple murder case. Police have recently moved the court to seek narco-analysis and brain mapping tests for the primary accused as the probe enters its final stages.
