**Local News Bulletin: KR Puram, Bangalore**
**Date:** Friday, September 4, 2026

### **1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **New Flyover Restrictions:** Starting today, **September 4**, the Bangalore Traffic Police have implemented a **ban on two-wheelers and autos** on five major elevated corridors (KIA/Airport Road, Electronic City, BGS, Peenya, and Silk Board-Ragigudda). While the KR Puram Hanging Bridge is not currently on this list, expect diverted traffic and increased congestion on the ground-level roads at Tin Factory as a result of these city-wide changes.
*   **Current Snarls:** Slow-moving traffic is reported at **Tin Factory towards Kasturi Nagar** due to a heavy vehicle breakdown earlier this morning. Commuters heading towards Hebbal should expect an additional 15–20 minute delay.
*   **Hanging Bridge:** Automated signals at the mouth of the bridge are operational to manage flow from Mahadevapura and Hosakote. Avoid the stretch between 5:30 PM and 8:30 PM if possible.

### **2. Civic Alerts & Infrastructure**
*   **Water Supply:** No major disruptions are reported for today. However, residents are advised to store water in advance for a **60-hour scheduled shutdown** from **September 15 to 17, 2026**, for Cauvery Stage V maintenance.
*   **Power:** No specific power cuts are scheduled for KR Puram today, following the completion of maintenance in nearby HBR Layout yesterday. 
*   **Civic News:** The National Land Monetisation Corporation has extended the bid deadline for the **44-acre ITI Limited land parcel** in KR Puram to September 28. The state government continues to object to the sale, which may impact future development in the area.

### **3. Festival Alert: Holi vs. Janmashtami**
*   **Holi 2026:** Please note that **Holi was celebrated on March 4, 2026**. There are no Holi events occurring today.
*   **Today’s Celebration:** Today is **Sri Krishna Janmashtami** (September 4, 2026). 
    *   **Local Event:** A major "Krishna Darshan" celebration including cultural programs and *Maha Prasadam* is taking place today at the **S-E-A College Auditorium, KR Puram**.
    *   **ISKCON Bangalore:** Grand celebrations are also ongoing at ISKCON (North Bangalore) and various satellite centers; expect heavy footfall and traffic near temple zones.
