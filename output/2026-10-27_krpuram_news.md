**KR Puram Resident Bulletin – October 27, 2026**

### 🚥 Traffic & Infrastructure
*   **Hanging Bridge (Cable-Stayed Bridge):** Structural concerns have been highlighted recently regarding the 23-year-old bridge. Residents should watch for uneven surfaces and potential maintenance work. Expect slow-moving traffic towards Tin Factory due to the ongoing structural health monitoring and existing bottlenecks near the railway station.
*   **Tin Factory Junction:** Congestion remains high. Commuters are advised to use the opened foot-over-bridge (FOB) connecting the KR Puram Metro to the Railway Station to avoid crossing at grade, which remains a primary cause of traffic snarls in the area.
*   **Weather Impact:** An impending "Very Severe Cyclonic Storm" near the Andhra coast may bring overcast skies and isolated heavy rainfall to Bengaluru today. Expect sudden traffic slowdowns if rain intensifies during peak evening hours.
