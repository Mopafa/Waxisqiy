### **KR Puram Local News Bulletin: September 16, 2026**

#### **1. Traffic Alert: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Festive Return Congestion:** Heavy traffic is reported at the **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge** junction today as commuters return to the city following the Ganesha Chaturthi break. Peak congestion is expected to last until **12:00 PM**, particularly on the route towards Whitefield and ITPL.
*   **Rain Havoc:** Following heavy downpours last night (Sept 15), residual waterlogging at the KR Puram bottleneck and Lowry Underpass is slowing vehicle movement. Traffic police recommend using the **Metro (Purple Line)** or alternate routes via **Kasturi Nagar** to avoid the 30-40 minute delays currently seen at the cable bridge.

#### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Major Water Cut:** The BWSSB has announced a **24-hour Cauvery water supply shutdown** starting today at **6:00 AM (Sept 16)** until **6:00 AM tomorrow (Sept 17)**. This affects all areas dependent on the Cauvery Project for maintenance. Residents are advised to use stored water judiciously.
*   **Liquor Ban:** A city-wide **liquor ban** is in effect starting today, **September 16, through September 20**, to maintain order during the Ganesha idol immersion processions.
*   **Weather Warning:** The IMD has issued a **yellow alert** for Bengaluru today, forecasting moderate to heavy rainfall. Low-lying areas like Sai Layout and ITI Colony should remain on alert for potential flooding.
*   **Power Supply:** While major maintenance is focused on the Koramangala and Hebbal divisions today, localized power fluctuations are possible in KR Puram due to rain-related repair work.

#### **3. Festival Update: Holi 2026 vs. Today’s Events**
*   **Holi 2026 Clarification:** Please note that **Holi was celebrated on March 4, 2026**. There are no Holi celebrations scheduled for today, September 16.
*   **Today’s Observances:** Residents are instead celebrating **Balarama Jayanti** and **Vishwakarma Puja**. 
*   **Ganesh Chaturthi:** Immersion festivities continue across KR Puram. Local processions near the KR Puram lake may lead to temporary road diversions in the evening.

**Resident Advice:** Expect significant delays on Old Madras Road. Ensure water tanks are filled and plan commutes early to avoid rain-triggered gridlocks in the evening.
