**KR Puram Local News Bulletin – Monday, August 3, 2026**

---

### **1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Tin Factory Junction:** Expect persistent slow-moving traffic throughout the day. Major redevelopment work on the **17-km Outer Ring Road (ORR) stretch** from KR Puram to Central Silk Board was officially approved on August 1. Residents can expect ongoing preparatory work, including road widening and utility shifting, which may cause intermittent lane closures.
*   **Beniganahalli/Hanging Bridge:** Traffic remains congested near the Beniganahalli junction due to ongoing **railway bridge repair works**. Commuters traveling toward KR Puram and Mahadevapura are advised to use the service roads or take a left toward Kasturinagar to avoid the bottleneck under the railway overpass. 
*   **Metro Progress:** Construction for the **Namma Metro Blue Line** continues at a steady pace. The Silk Board-KR Pura section is currently on track for a late 2026 opening, though active piling work near the Hanging Bridge is contributing to peak-hour delays.

### **2. Civic Alerts & Local Infrastructure**
*   **Railway Service:** Night train services on the **Bengaluru-Mangaluru-Karwar** route are scheduled to resume today, August 3, following track restoration work by the South Western Railway (SWR).
*   **BBMP Maintenance:** There are no scheduled major power or water shutdowns reported specifically for KR Puram today. However, residents are advised to be cautious of potential waterlogging in **Whitecity Layout** and low-lying areas if afternoon thundershowers occur, following recent BBMP alerts on drain maintenance.
*   **Civic Safety:** BBMP has been active in clearing "growth" (trees/shrubs) from the KR Puram Hanging Bridge structures to ensure structural integrity and prevent accidents.

### **3. Festival Watch: Holi & Today’s Events**
*   **Holi 2026 Update:** Please note that **Holi 2026 celebrations concluded in March** (March 3–4, 2026). There are no Holi-related events occurring today.
*   **Today's Festival (Aadi Perukku):** Today, August 3, marks the celebration of **Aadi Perukku**, a festival honoring the life-sustaining properties of water. Local temples in and around KR Puram and the Kaveri basin areas may see increased footfall for special prayers and rituals.
*   **Other Events:** The **Bangalore International Centre (BIC)** is hosting the final day of the "Architecture: Structure and Light" photography exhibition today (11:00 AM – 9:00 PM).

**Travel Tip:** To avoid the worst of the Tin Factory gridlock, residents are encouraged to use the **Purple Line Metro** between Whitefield and KR Puram, which remains the fastest alternative to the congested ORR.
