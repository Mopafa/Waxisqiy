### **KR Puram Local News Bulletin: October 18, 2026**

#### **1. Traffic Alert: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **High Congestion:** Expect severe delays at the **Tin Factory Junction** and the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge (Cable-Stayed Bridge)**. Traffic is currently "crawling" due to the **Dasara long weekend** exodus. Many residents are heading toward the highways, and transit hubs are experiencing heavy passenger volume.
*   **Recent Accidents:** Local police have reported multiple fatal accidents in the KR Puram traffic limits over the last 24 hours. Notably, two men were killed at Bhattarahalli (near KR Puram) at 2:00 AM after a collision with a truck. Motorists are advised to exercise extreme caution, especially during night-time travel.
*   **Weather Impact:** Intermittent rain and lingering waterlogging from earlier in the week have slowed speeds on the Outer Ring Road (ORR) stretch from KR Puram toward Marathahalli.

#### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Infrastructure Concerns:** A recent structural assessment of the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge** has raised concerns regarding vegetation growing through cracks and potential moisture ingress. While the bridge remains open, commuters may notice maintenance crews in the area.
*   **Noise Disturbances:** Residents are cautioned about festive noise levels. Civic authorities have received numerous complaints regarding late-night loudspeakers and Dandiya celebrations. Local healthcare experts have issued advisories regarding the impact of prolonged high-decibel noise on residents.
*   **Water & Lighting:** BBMP has resolved the water stagnation issue at the nearby KR Circle underpass. Additionally, a city-wide deadline of **October 31** has been set to replace all faulty LED streetlights in the KR Puram area.

#### **3. Festival Update: Holi vs. Dasara**
*   **Calendar Correction:** Please note that **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 3–4, 2026**. There are no Holi celebrations occurring today.
*   **Current Festivities:** The KR Puram area is currently celebrating **Dasara and Durga Puja (October 15–21)**. Major events are organized by the local Bengali and Gujarati communities, featuring **Dandiya and Puja pandals** in and around Mahadevapura and North Bangalore.

**Commuter Tip:** If traveling toward the city center or Kolar/Hoskote, use the Metro to bypass the Tin Factory bottleneck if possible. Check Google Maps for real-time ETAs as travel times are currently 30–45 minutes higher than average.
