This bulletin provides a summary of local news, traffic, and civic updates for KR Puram, Bangalore, as of **Sunday, July 26, 2026**.

### **🚨 Local News & Metro Updates**
*   **Metro Blue Line Progress:** Greater Bengaluru Development Minister Krishna Byre Gowda has directed the **BMRCL to begin trial runs** for the Namma Metro Blue Line (connecting KR Puram to Central Silk Board and the Airport) in **October 2026**. Commercial operations are now slated for 2027.
*   **ORR Redevelopment:** During a meeting with stakeholders yesterday, the Minister confirmed a fund of **₹450 crore** for the redevelopment of the Outer Ring Road (ORR) stretch. Public suggestions are being invited for the design to manage the area’s "junction complexity."

### **🚦 Traffic Watch: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Current Status:** Chronic congestion continues at the **Tin Factory junction** and the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge (Cable-Stayed Bridge)**. The area remains a primary focus for traffic management due to complex merging lanes from the ORR and Old Madras Road.
*   **Infrastructure Issues:** Residents are advised to stay cautious on the Hanging Bridge due to reports of uneven surfaces and damaged concrete railings, which have been temporarily barricaded by traffic police. No new road closures are reported for today.

### **⚡ Civic Alerts: Power & Water**
*   **Power:** While BESCOM announced scheduled maintenance for today (July 26) between **10:00 AM and 5:00 PM**, major outages are primarily concentrated in **western and southern suburbs** (such as Tavarekere and Byadarahalli). No specific wide-scale power cuts have been issued for the KR Puram/East zone today.
*   **Water:** No specific water supply disruptions are scheduled for today. However, the KR Puram area remains under a general "high-alert" for water stress this season, with continued reliance on private tankers in certain pockets.

### **🎨 Holi 2026 Status**
*   **Celebration Note:** Please note that **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 3rd and 4th, 2026**. There are no Holi celebrations occurring today, July 26. Previous events at local venues like Grand Mercure Gopalan Mall and VRR Convention Centre concluded in March.

**Resident Tip:** With the Minister's recent focus on the ORR, expect increased presence of traffic personnel and potential survey activity near the KR Puram metro interchange today.
