**KR Puram Local News Bulletin: November 1, 2026**

### **1. Today’s Major Event: Kannada Rajyotsava**
While you asked about Holi celebrations, please note that **Holi 2026 took place on March 4**. Today, November 1, is **Kannada Rajyotsava (Karnataka Formation Day)**. 
*   **Celebrations:** Expect widespread local festivities across KR Puram, including flag hoisting ceremonies, cultural processions, and Kannada music performances at major junctions and residential layouts like T.C. Palya and Akshaya Nagar.
*   **Public Holiday:** Most government offices, schools, and banks are closed today.

### **2. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Congestion Status:** Moderate to heavy traffic is reported at the Tin Factory junction. While the typical "office rush" is lower due to the public holiday, localized congestion is expected near the KR Puram Hanging Bridge due to Rajyotsava processions.
*   **Metro Impact:** The Blue Line construction near KR Pura continues to affect service road widths. Commuters heading toward Hebbal or Whitefield are advised to use the main flyover rather than service roads to avoid delays.
*   **Commuter Alert:** Check for slow-moving traffic near the KR Puram Railway Station as many travelers are returning from the long weekend.

### **3. Civic Alerts: 'Ward One' Campaign Starts Today**
The BBMP has launched the **'Ward One' campaign** starting today, November 1, which includes **KR Pura (Ward 14)**. 
*   **Focus:** For the next 30 days, local authorities will focus on intensive pothole repairs, waste removal, and streetlight maintenance in the ward.
*   **Resident Action:** Residents are encouraged to report specific civic grievances (like uncleared garbage or broken streetlights) to the ward office or via the Sahaya portal, as these will be prioritized during this month-long drive.

### **4. Local Incident Report**
*   **Railway Bridge Work:** While the Lowry Under Bridge repairs (previously scheduled) have concluded, remain alert for any residual diversions on the ITI Colony side.
*   **Land Allotment News:** A recent High Court ruling has directed the BDA to finalize the allotment of developed sites for landowners who relinquished land for the Hebbal-KR Puram Outer Ring Road. This may affect local real estate documentation for properties in the Nagavara/KR Puram stretch.

**Quick Tip for Residents:** With Kannada Rajyotsava events peaking this evening, avoid the Old Madras Road stretch between 5:00 PM and 8:00 PM to bypass heavy pedestrian crowds and slow-moving floats.
