**KR PURAM LOCAL BULLETIN: SEPTEMBER 3, 2026**

### **1. Traffic & Transit Updates**
*   **Tin Factory / Hanging Bridge:** Expect heavy congestion today due to **Sri Krishna Janmashtami** celebrations across the city. While the main diversions are near ISKCON (Malleshwaram), the surge in festive commuters is impacting major arteries like Old Madras Road and the Outer Ring Road.
*   **Flyover Ban Alert (Effective Tomorrow):** A major new restriction begins **September 4**. Two-wheelers and autos will be banned from five key flyovers, including the **KIA (Airport) Elevated Corridor** and **Electronic City Flyover**. Plan alternative routes starting tomorrow.
*   **Metro Progress:** Work on the **Blue Line** (KR Puram to Airport) continues. Residents are advised to avoid the service roads near the KR Puram metro station during peak hours (5:00 PM – 8:00 PM) due to ongoing structural work.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Scheduled Power Outage:** BESCOM has announced a power cut today from **12:00 PM to 4:00 PM** in parts of KR Puram and surrounding areas (including HBR Layout, Krishna Reddy Layout, and Teachers Colony) for network maintenance.
*   **Water Supply:** No disruption today; however, BWSSB has issued a city-wide warning for a major **60-hour water shutdown** from **September 15 to 17** for Cauvery Stage V maintenance. Residents should plan storage in advance.
*   **Crime Alert:** Local police are investigating a fatal stabbing of a 21-year-old man in KR Puram reported earlier today. Increased patrolling is expected in the vicinity of the railway station and market area.
