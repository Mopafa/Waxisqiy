### **Local News Bulletin: KR Puram & Bengaluru East**
**Date:** September 18, 2026

#### **1. Traffic Alert: Tin Factory / Hanging Bridge**
*   **Current Status:** Expect **heavy slow-moving traffic** throughout the day at the Tin Factory junction and Hanging Bridge. Congestion is intensified by multiple Ganesha idol immersion processions moving toward Halasuru (Ulsoor) Lake.
*   **Diversions:** Major restrictions are in place in nearby **Banaswadi**, **KG Halli**, and **Pulakeshinagar**. 
    *   Vehicles from **Thanisandra** toward the city are advised to take a left at Nagawara Junction and proceed via Hennur Junction to avoid procession routes.
    *   Nagawara Main Road and Tannery Road are restricted until 4:00 AM tomorrow (Sept 19).

#### **2. Civic Alerts: Power & Water**
*   **Power (BESCOM):** A scheduled **9-hour power outage** (9:00 AM – 6:00 PM) is affecting several northern and eastern pockets today due to KPTCL maintenance. While KR Puram’s main residential blocks are not on the primary list, neighboring **Whitefield** and **Indiranagar** divisions may see intermittent disruptions.
*   **Water (BWSSB):** Following the major 60-hour maintenance shutdown earlier this week, BWSSB confirms that **Cauvery water supply has resumed** as of yesterday morning. Pressure may take another 24 hours to fully normalize in high-reach areas of KR Puram.

#### **3. Festival Update: Ganesha Visarjan vs. Holi**
*   **Holi 2026:** Please note that **Holi was celebrated on March 4, 2026**. There are no Holi festivities today.
*   **Ganesha Immersion (Today's Focus):** Today is a major day for **Ganesha Visarjan**. 
    *   **Immersion Points:** Residents can use designated mobile tankers at KR Puram ward levels or the kalyani at **Halasuru Lake**. 
    *   **Prohibition:** Plaster of Paris (PoP) idols are strictly banned. Only eco-friendly/clay idols are permitted in designated water bodies.

#### **4. Infrastructure News**
*   **Bridge Maintenance:** Recent structural assessments of the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge** have identified concerns regarding vegetation growth in cracks and reinforcement corrosion. Commuters are advised to stay updated on potential upcoming maintenance-related lane closures.
