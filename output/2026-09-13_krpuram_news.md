### **KR Puram Local News Bulletin: September 13, 2026**

**1. Traffic Alert: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Status:** **Severe Congestion.** As of this afternoon, the stretch from Tin Factory to the KR Puram Hanging Bridge is witnessing heavy tailbacks. 
*   **Reason:** High volume of commuters heading out of the city for the **Ganesh Chaturthi** long weekend. KSRTC has deployed over 2,400 special buses, many of which are passing through this corridor towards Kolar and Hoskote.
*   **Commuter Advice:** Expect delays of **40–60 minutes** at the junction. Traffic police recommend using the **SMVT Baiyappanahalli** route or the Outer Ring Road service lanes to avoid the main bottleneck if traveling toward North Bangalore.

**2. Civic Alerts & Restrictions**
*   **Flyover Rules:** Reminder that the city-wide vehicle restrictions on major flyovers (effective since Sept 4) remain in place. Heavy goods vehicles are restricted during peak hours (7 AM–11 AM & 4 PM–10 PM) on the Old Madras Road stretch to manage festival traffic.
*   **BBMP Guidelines:** For residents planning installations tomorrow, BBMP has strictly prohibited **Plaster of Paris (PoP)** idols. Local authorities in KR Puram will be monitoring neighborhood stalls.
*   **Public Safety:** Police presence has been increased around the KR Puram market area to manage the large crowds gathered for festival shopping.

**3. Festival Update: Holi vs. Ganesh Chaturthi**
*   **Note for Residents:** While you asked about Holi celebrations, **Holi 2026 took place on March 4, 2026**.
*   **Today’s Event:** Today is the **Eve of Ganesh Chaturthi** (which officially begins tomorrow, Sept 14). 
*   **Local Happenings:** KR Puram markets (near the railway station and ITI colony) are currently the hub for festival preparations. Expect vibrant flower markets and idol stalls, but be prepared for heavy pedestrian footfall and limited parking.

**Concise Summary for Residents:**
If you are planning to travel through the Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge area this evening, **leave early**. The "festival exodus" is in full swing. Celebrate safely by using eco-friendly Ganesha idols for tomorrow's festivities.
