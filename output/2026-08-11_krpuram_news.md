**KR PURAM LOCAL BULLETIN: AUGUST 11, 2026**

### **🚦 Traffic & Commute Report**
*   **ORR Redevelopment Alert:** Major congestion is expected on the **Silk Board–KR Puram stretch** starting today. A year-long project to replace asphalt with concrete began yesterday (August 10). Lane closures and diversions are in effect; expect significantly higher travel times between Marathahalli and KR Puram.
*   **Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge:** Chronic bottlenecks persist at the Tin Factory junction due to ongoing metro work and bus stops at the bridge's mouth. Commuters report 15–20 minute delays during peak hours.
*   **Bennaganahalli Bridge:** Railway repair works on the overbridge continue to cause slow-moving traffic towards KR Puram and Mahadevapura. These repairs are expected to conclude by the end of August.
