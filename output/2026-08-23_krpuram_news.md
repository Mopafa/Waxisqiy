**KR Puram Local News Bulletin: Sunday, August 23, 2026**

### **1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Bridge Maintenance Alert:** Residents are advised that structural concerns have been raised regarding the **KR Puram Cable-Stayed Bridge**. Reports as of yesterday (August 22) highlight vegetation growing in structural cracks and potential reinforcement corrosion. While the bridge remains open, commuters may notice inspection teams or minor maintenance work.
*   **Fit India Run Congestion:** Early morning traffic near **ITI Gate Road** was impacted by the "Fit India Bengaluru Run 2026," which commenced at 5:00 AM today. Minor residual delays may persist in the vicinity of Dooravani Nagar.
*   **Ongoing Metro Work:** The **Blue Line (Airport Line)** construction continues to cause bottlenecks at the Tin Factory junction. Recent updates confirm that the KR Puram-Silk Board stretch is now slated for a mid-2027 opening, meaning the current diversions and narrow carriageways will remain for the foreseeable future.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Footpath Clearance Drive:** The Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) has issued a directive to complete the clearance of all footpath encroachments in the KR Puram area by the end of August. Repeat offenders face double penalties, and officials are actively seizing materials from encroached sub-arterial roads.
*   **Weather & Flooding:** Following intermittent spells of rain this week, a **Yellow Alert** remains in place for Bengaluru. Residents in low-lying areas, particularly **Sai Layout, Pai Layout, and Naggapa Reddy Layout**, are advised to stay vigilant as localized flooding was reported earlier this weekend.
*   **New Parking Rules:** Draft notifications for the **Greater Bengaluru Authority (Parking) Rules, 2026** are now under review. Residents should prepare for the eventual implementation of paid on-street parking in residential stretches and commercial hubs across KR Puram.
