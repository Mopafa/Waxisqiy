### **KR Puram Local News Bulletin: September 19, 2026**

#### **1. Traffic & Infrastructure Update**
*   **Tin Factory Junction:** Expect heavy congestion throughout the day due to ongoing **elevated metro construction** (linking the ORR-Airport line). Commuters are advised to add 20–30 minutes to their travel time during peak hours (8:30 AM – 11:00 AM and 5:30 PM – 8:30 PM).
*   **Hanging Bridge (Cable-Stayed Bridge):** The bridge remains open to light and heavy vehicles, but authorities have issued a **structural advisory**. Recent inspections highlighted vegetation growth and reinforcement corrosion in the 23-year-old structure. Motorists are cautioned about uneven surfaces and minor potholes on the stretch toward ITI Colony.
*   **Alternative Route:** Residents heading toward Ramamurthy Nagar or ITPL should consider using the **KR Puram Metro feeder services** or the newly repaired railway underpass near ITI Depo to bypass the main junction gridlock.

#### **2. Civic Alerts & Governance**
*   **Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA):** Residents are reminded that civic administration has officially transitioned from BBMP to the **GBA East Corporation**. Local ward offices are now operating under this new decentralized structure for property tax and grievance redressal.
*   **Power Supply:** No major scheduled outages are reported for KR Puram today. (Note: A large-scale outage affected northern Bengaluru/Yelahanka yesterday).
*   **Water Supply:** BWSSB has confirmed that water supply is **normal**. The emergency maintenance on the Cauvery Stage V pipeline was completed on September 17, and pressure has stabilized across East Bengaluru.

*   **Holi 2026:** Please note that **Holi was celebrated on March 4, 2026**. There are no Holi-related celebrations today as the festival is traditionally a spring event.
