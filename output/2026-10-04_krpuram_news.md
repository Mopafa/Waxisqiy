**KR PURAM LOCAL NEWS BULLETIN**
**Date:** Sunday, October 4, 2026  
**Location:** KR Puram / Tin Factory Area, Bengaluru

### **1. Traffic & Infrastructure Update**
*   **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge:** Commuters should expect the usual heavy congestion at the Tin Factory junction. Structural concerns have been raised recently regarding the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge** due to vegetation growth in cracks and potential moisture ingress; while it remains open, authorities are under pressure to conduct a comprehensive structural health assessment.
*   **Metro Extension News:** A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is currently being prepared for a new **Metro line from KR Puram to Hoskote**. This 11-station corridor is expected to include stops at ITI Bhavan, TC Palya, and Budigere Cross, aimed at easing the long-term traffic burden on NH-75.
*   **Commuter Advice:** Peak hour speeds remain low (approx. 13–15 km/h). Use the foot-over-bridge (FOB) connecting the KR Puram Metro to the Railway Station to avoid hazardous at-grade crossings.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Weather Warning:** The IMD and local weather stations have forecast a **thunderstorm this evening** (Oct 4). Residents are advised to clear terrace drains and avoid parking vehicles under trees or near old structures. 
*   **Drainage Maintenance:** BBMP has been alerted by residents to persistent waterlogging issues near stormwater drains in East Bengaluru following recent rains. Emergency teams are on standby for low-lying areas.

### **3. Holi 2026 Update**
*   **Clarification:** Please note that **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 4, 2026**. There are no Holi celebrations or public events scheduled for today, October 4. Local residents are instead observing the typical Sunday market activity and weekend religious services at local temples like the Mahabaleshwara Temple.

### **Summary for Residents:**
Expect a rainy evening; plan your commute through Tin Factory earlier in the afternoon to avoid the combination of peak Sunday traffic and potential rain-induced gridlock. Stay tuned for further updates on the KR Puram-Hoskote Metro alignment.
