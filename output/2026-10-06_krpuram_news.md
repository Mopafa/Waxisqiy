# KR Puram Local News Bulletin: October 6, 2026

**Status:** LIVE Updates | **Location:** KR Puram, Bangalore

### 🌊 Civic Alert: Severe Flooding & Waterlogging
Following heavy rainfall on Monday evening (Oct 5), residents in KR Puram are facing significant civic distress today.
*   **Manjunatha Nagar (Kalkere Ward):** Residents are reporting severe flooding with water reaching depths of **2 to 3 feet**. Rainwater and sewage have entered homes along the 2nd Cross, damaging household goods. Local sumps are reportedly contaminated.
*   **Drainage Concerns:** Residents have raised complaints regarding alleged encroachments on stormwater drains in the area, which have worsened the inundation.

### 🚦 Traffic Report: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge
Commuters should expect significantly higher travel times today due to the aftermath of the rain.
*   **Tin Factory Junction:** Persistent waterlogging near the KR Puram Metro station and Tin Factory has slowed movement on **Old Madras Road**. Visibility was reported low during earlier showers; motorists are advised to use headlights and drive cautiously.
*   **KR Puram Hanging Bridge:** Traffic is crawling on the cable-stayed bridge. While automated signals are in place to streamline flow from Mahadevapura and Hoskote, the "choke point" at the down-ramp towards Tin Factory remains critical. 
*   **Structural Note:** Recent engineering assessments have flagged concerns regarding vegetation and minor cracks on the 23-year-old Hanging Bridge. While it remains open, heavy vehicle loading continues to be monitored.

### ⚡ Utility Alerts
*   **Power:** While no specific localized blackout is currently active for the KR Puram core today, BESCOM maintenance is ongoing in North Bengaluru. Residents are advised to keep devices charged as monsoon-related line faults are frequent.
*   **Water:** No major BWSSB shutdowns are reported for KR Puram today; however, those in flooded zones are urged to boil water due to potential sump contamination.
