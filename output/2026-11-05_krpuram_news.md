**Local News Bulletin: KR Puram, Bengaluru**
**Date:** Thursday, November 5, 2026

### **1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Current Status:** Traffic at the **Tin Factory junction** and the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge** remains slow-moving during peak hours, consistent with its status as a major bottleneck. 
*   **Signal Monitoring:** Residents are advised to account for an additional **15–20 minutes** when crossing from Mahadevapura or Kolar-Hosakote toward Hebbal/Indiranagar due to the automated signal system at the bridge's mouth.
*   **Route Alert:** The **ITI Colony/Lowry Under Bridge** area is clear of major repairs (which were completed earlier this year), but commuters are urged to use the **Cable-Stayed Bridge** as the primary route to bypass the narrow service roads near the railway station.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **BMTC Fare Hike:** A major civic change takes effect **today (Nov 5)**. BMTC bus fares have been hiked by an average of **35%**. The minimum fare has increased from **₹6 to ₹10**. Commuters are advised to carry the correct change or use digital passes to avoid delays.
*   **Power Supply:** According to the latest BESCOM updates for today, there are **no scheduled power cuts** for the KR Puram/Mahadevapura zone. However, local maintenance-related fluctuations may occur in **TC Palya** and **Sairam Layout**.
*   **Water Supply:** No major BWSSB disruptions are reported for today. Residents are reminded that groundwater levels in KR Puram and Mahadevapura are predicted to drop significantly by early next year, and judicious water use is encouraged.
