## **KR Puram Local News Bulletin: Wednesday, August 26, 2026**

**1. Traffic & Transit: Tin Factory / Hanging Bridge**
*   **Congestion Alert:** Expect significant delays at the **Tin Factory junction** and the **Hanging Bridge (KR Puram Cable Bridge)** today. Heavy congestion is anticipated city-wide due to processions and large-scale gatherings for **Eid-e-Milad-un-Nabi**. While the main gatherings are at YMCA ground (12 PM – 11 PM), the ripple effect is expected to hit the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and Old Madras Road (OMR) bottlenecks.
*   **Bridge Condition:** Authorities continue to monitor the 23-year-old KR Puram cable-stayed bridge following recent structural concerns regarding concrete cracking and vegetation growth. No new weight restrictions are in place today, but slow-moving traffic remains the norm.
*   **Metro Update:** Construction on the Blue Line (Phase 2A) connecting KR Puram to Central Silk Board is progressing toward its target December 2026 opening.

**2. Civic Alerts: Power & Water**
*   **BESCOM (Power):** Scheduled maintenance is taking place today across several Bengaluru feeders. While KR Puram is not on the primary "all-day" shutdown list, residents should be prepared for potential staggered outages between **10:00 AM and 5:00 PM**. 
*   **BWSSB (Water):** There is no major water shutdown scheduled for today. However, residents are advised to store water for **Friday, August 28**, when emergency repairs on the Cauvery Water Supply Scheme (Stage 4, Phase 2) will cause city-wide disruptions.
*   **Local Crime News:** Updates in the Seegehalli triple murder case (within KR Puram limits) indicate that a key accused techie has sought "approver status" as of late August; police investigations are ongoing.
