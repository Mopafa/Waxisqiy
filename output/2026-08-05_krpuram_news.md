**KR Puram Resident Bulletin – August 5, 2026**

### **🚦 Traffic & Commute Update**
*   **Bennaganahalli & Tin Factory:** Expect heavy congestion on the Old Madras Road (OMR) between Bennaganahalli and Tin Factory. Traffic is significantly backed up due to ongoing **railway bridge repair works**, which are scheduled to continue throughout August.
*   **Hanging Bridge:** Slow vehicle movement is reported on the KR Puram Suspension Bridge (Hanging Bridge) during peak hours. Residents heading towards ITI Colony or Ramamurthy Nagar are advised to use the cable-stayed bridge to bypass current bottlenecks.
*   **Metro Impact:** Work on the **Blue Line (Airport Line)** continues, with the KR Puram–Hebbal stretch identified as a high-congestion zone due to active construction. Commuters should factor in an additional 20–30 minutes for travel toward Hebbal.

### **📢 Civic Alerts**
*   **Water Adalat (Tomorrow):** BWSSB will hold a **Water Adalat on Thursday, August 6**, from 9:30 AM to 11:00 AM. Residents of KR Puram and Mahadevapura can visit their local sub-division offices to resolve issues related to water bills, new connections, or sewerage complaints.
*   **Waste Clearance:** Under the "Freedom From Waste" campaign, the GBA has cleared over 250 tonnes of debris in KR Puram and Chinnappanahalli this week. Residents are urged to report illegal dumping via official channels.
*   **Water Restrictions:** Reminder that BWSSB’s mandatory **aerator installation** rule is in effect. Using potable water for vehicle washing or gardening remains prohibited and may attract a ₹5,000 fine.

### **🌸 Events & Festivals**
*   **Holi 2026:** Note that the festival of **Holi was celebrated on March 3–4, 2026**, and there are no official Holi events today.
*   **Independence Day Flower Show:** The 220th **Lalbagh Flower Show** begins today (August 5) and will run until August 15. Expect increased traffic on routes leading toward the city center.
*   **Local Event:** The **GoSwadeshi UTSAV** is happening today at Jayamahal Palace, featuring handloom and local crafts, for those looking for community events nearby.

**Resident Advice:** Monitor the Bengaluru Traffic Police (BTP) social media feeds for real-time diversions, as peak-hour rain could further impact the Tin Factory junction.
