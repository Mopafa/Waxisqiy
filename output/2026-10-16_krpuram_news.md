**Bulletin for KR Puram Residents – Friday, October 16, 2026**

### **1. Traffic & Transit Update**
*   **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge:** Traffic is currently **slow-moving** with average speeds recorded at **12–15 km/h**. Major bottlenecks are reported at the Tin Factory junction due to the ongoing construction of the Bengaluru Metro Blue Line (Silk Board–Airport) and residual waterlogging from last night’s storm.
*   **Weather Impact:** A massive thunderstorm last night caused significant **waterlogging** on the service road between Kalyan Nagar and Tin Factory, as well as near the KR Puram railway station under-bridge. Commuters heading toward ITPL and Mahadevapura should expect an additional 20–30 minutes of travel time.
*   **Metro Update:** Officials have confirmed that the full 58 km Blue Line, which will connect KR Puram to the airport, is on track for a 2028 completion, though some stretches may see earlier trials.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Rain Alert:** The IMD has issued a continued alert for intermittent showers today. BBMP civic teams are currently stationed at "flood-prone" spots in the East Zone (KR Puram, Hoodi, and Kasturi Nagar) to address blocked drains and tree-fall incidents.
*   **Civic Enforcement:** BBMP has intensified its drive against civic violations in the neighboring markets. Residents are reminded that fines are being strictly imposed for the use of banned plastics and improper waste disposal.
*   **Power Supply:** While no city-wide outages are scheduled, localized maintenance due to storm damage may cause short interruptions. Check the BESCOM portal for specific ward-level updates.

### **3. Festival Spotlight: Durga Puja & Navaratri 2026**
*While Holi 2026 concluded in March, today marks the start of the major October festive season in Bangalore.*
*   **Maha Shashti (Durga Puja):** Today, October 16, is **Maha Shashti**, the first day of the Durga Puja festival. 
    *   **Local Pandal:** The **Aamantran Welfare Association Trust** in Basavanapura, KR Puram, is hosting its annual Pujo. Highlights include traditional "Mahila Dhakis" (women dhol players) invited from West Bengal.
    *   **Nearby:** The Whitefield Cultural Association (Nallurahalli Ground) and celebrations at Phoenix Marketcity are expected to draw large crowds starting this evening.
*   **Navaratri/Dandiya:** The **"Rangilo 2026"** Grand Dandiya & Garba Festival begins today at Legend Microbrewery and other venues in the Whitefield/KR Puram corridor, featuring live DJ sets and traditional dhol.

**Resident Tip:** If you are planning to visit Durga Puja pandals or Dandiya events this evening, use public transport or carpool where possible, as traffic near KR Puram and Whitefield is expected to peak between 6:00 PM and 9:00 PM.
