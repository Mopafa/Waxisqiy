**LOCAL BULLETIN: KR PURAM, BANGALORE**
**Date:** Saturday, August 29, 2026

### **1. Traffic & Commute Alert**
*   **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge:** Heavy congestion is expected throughout the day. Ongoing **railway bridge work** on Coca-Cola Godown Road (Benniganahalli to Old Madras Road) continues to necessitate a 90-day traffic diversion that began in June.
*   **St. Mary’s Feast Impact:** While centered in Shivajinagar, the annual feast starting today has led to major city-wide traffic diversions. BMTC buses heading toward **KR Puram, Marathahalli, and Whitefield** may not use the Shivajinagar Bus Stand and are instead operating from temporary stops on **Cubbon Road** (between Kamaraj Road and Cubbon Road junction).
*   **New Signal:** Motorists coming from Mahadevapura and Kolar are advised to account for the automated signal at the mouth of the KR Puram Hanging Bridge, installed to regulate the high volume of crisscrossing traffic.

### **2. Civic Alerts**
*   **Scheduled Power Outage:** BESCOM has announced maintenance-related power cuts for today, **August 29, 2026**. Affected areas include **KR Puram, Hoodi, Whitefield, ITPL, and Mahadevapura**. Residents should prepare for potential disruptions between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
*   **Water Supply:** No specific alerts for water supply interruptions have been issued today for the Mahadevapura zone, but residents are advised to store extra water due to the scheduled power outages affecting pumping systems.

### **3. Festivals & Events**
*   **Holi 2026 Clarification:** Please note that **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 4**. There are no official Holi festivities scheduled for today.
*   **St. Mary’s Feast (Shivajinagar):** The 11-day festival begins today with the official flag-hoisting ceremony and evening processions. This will significantly increase crowds in central Bangalore.
*   **Gokulashtami (Utsav 2026):** Major cultural celebrations and Carnatic music concerts are being held today and tomorrow at Odukathur Mutt near Ulsoor Lake.
*   **Local Concerts:** 
    *   **The Yellow Diary** is performing live at The Humming Tree (Indiranagar) at 6:15 PM.
    *   **Rajesh Krishnan** is performing at Forum South Bengaluru at 6:00 PM.
    *   **Emraan Hashmi** is scheduled for a "Superstar Night" at Sunburn Union (Koramangala) starting at 7:00 PM.

**Resident Advice:** Avoid travel through central Bangalore (Shivajinagar/MG Road) after 2:00 PM due to feast-related processions. Use the **KR Puram Metro** to bypass Tin Factory bottlenecks where possible.
