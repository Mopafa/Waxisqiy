This local news bulletin is for residents of **KR Puram, Bangalore**, for **Saturday, August 1, 2026**.

### **🚦 Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Persistent Bottleneck:** Expect heavy congestion at the **Tin Factory junction** and the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge**. Morning commuters report slow-moving traffic as vehicles converge from the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and Old Madras Road (OMR).
*   **Ongoing Diversions:** The traffic police have maintained a congestion alert for the **Bennaganahalli-KR Puram stretch** due to lingering railway repair works at the overbridge. 
*   **Recommended Routes:** To avoid the worst of the Tin Factory backup, use the service road of the ORR or take a turn at **Kasturinagar** towards the Baiyappanahalli Metro station.
*   **Night Works:** Be aware of late-night Metro-related "height works" (11:00 PM – 5:00 AM) that may cause periodic lane closures on the stretch towards Hebbal.

### **⚡ Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Power Outages:** **BESCOM** has announced scheduled power cuts today, August 1, affecting parts of the **Whitefield Zone** (including peripheral KR Puram areas). Maintenance and line-shifting work are expected between **10:00 AM and 5:00 PM**.
*   **"Freedom from Waste" Drive:** Today marks the launch of the city-wide **"Just Clean Bengaluru" (JCB)** initiative. Expect heavy vehicle movement as the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) begins clearing an estimated 22,000 tonnes of construction debris and legacy waste from public spaces and roadsides.
*   **Water Usage Restrictions:** **BWSSB** continues its ban on the use of potable (drinking) water for washing vehicles or cleaning roads. Residents are encouraged to install aerators and use treated water for non-essential tasks to avoid fines of up to ₹5,000.

### **🎨 Holi 2026 Celebrations**
*   **Festival Status:** Please note that **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 3rd and 4th**. There are no Holi-themed events scheduled for today, August 1, as the festival is traditionally a spring celebration. Local community events today are primarily focused on the "Clean Bengaluru" campaign and standard weekend gatherings.

### **Resident Summary**
Plan for power disruptions until early evening and allow an extra 20–30 minutes for travel through the Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge corridor. If you have vacant plots or debris near your property, be aware that the GBA cleanup drive is active in the area starting today.
