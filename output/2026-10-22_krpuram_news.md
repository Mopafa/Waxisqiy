### **KR Puram Local Bulletin: October 22, 2026**

**1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Current Status:** Expect heavy congestion at the **Tin Factory junction** and the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge**. The bottleneck remains severe due to the automated signal at the mouth of the bridge, which has reportedly increased wait times for those coming from Mahadevapura and Hoskote.
*   **Advisory:** Commuters traveling toward Whitefield or the Outer Ring Road (ORR) are advised to use alternative routes through Kasturinagar or the Benniganahalli service road to bypass the main flyover pile-up. 
*   **Safety Note:** Recent local reports highlight the danger of over-speeding on the flyover; residents are urged to maintain cautious speeds following a fatal accident involving a minor near the divider earlier this year.

**2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Power (BESCOM):** Good news for residents—**no scheduled power outages** are reported for the KR Puram and Mahadevapura zones today. BESCOM has confirmed uninterrupted supply for the city.
*   **Water (BWSSB):** No major water disruptions are scheduled for today. However, following the recent post-monsoon maintenance in September, some tail-end areas may experience slight pressure fluctuations.
*   **Civic Works:** Be mindful of ongoing road widening projects on **Old Madras Road** near the KR Puram railway station, which may cause intermittent lane closures during off-peak hours.
