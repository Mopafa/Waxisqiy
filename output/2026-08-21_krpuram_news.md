**KR PURAM LOCAL NEWS BULLETIN: FRIDAY, AUGUST 21, 2026**

### **1. ⚠️ CRITICAL CIVIC ALERT: WATER SHUTDOWN TODAY**
*   **Event:** The **BWSSB** has scheduled a major 15-hour shutdown of the **Cauvery Water Supply Stage V** pumping stations.
*   **Time:** Today, **6:00 AM to 9:00 PM**.
*   **Reason:** Emergency maintenance to repair a leakage in the 3000mm pipeline valve.
*   **Impact:** Residents of KR Puram, Mahadevapura, and surrounding areas will experience a total disruption in water supply. Ensure you have stored sufficient water for the day.

*   **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge:** Expect the usual peak-hour congestion.
