**K.R. Puram Local News Bulletin: August 6, 2026**

### **Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Bottleneck Alert:** Commuters at the **Tin Factory Junction** and the **K.R. Puram Hanging Bridge** are advised to expect heavy congestion today. Ongoing construction for the **Blue Line Metro** (Phase 2A/2B) continues to restrict lane width. BMRCL has reaffirmed today that the Silk Board–K.R. Puram stretch is still on track for an end-2026 completion.
*   **Regional Diversions:** Trial traffic diversions have commenced today at **Madivala** (8:00 AM – 10:30 AM). While not in K.R. Puram, this may affect the flow of outbound traffic toward Hosur Road and secondary routes connecting to the Outer Ring Road.
*   **Railway Bridge Works:** Minor delays persist on the **Bennaganahalli–K.R. Puram stretch** due to intermittent maintenance at the railway overbridge.

### **Civic Alerts**
*   **Power Supply:** As of this morning, BESCOM has not issued a scheduled power shutdown specifically for K.R. Puram for August 6. However, localized maintenance is reported in parts of **Bannerghatta Road** and **North Bengaluru**. Residents are advised to keep emergency contact numbers ready for unscheduled monsoon-related outages.
*   **Water Supply:** No major BWSSB supply disruptions are scheduled for today. The recent 15-hour disruption from July 31 has been fully restored.
*   **Civic Unrest Alert:** Note that pro-Kannada groups have called for a **State Bandh on August 13, 2026**, regarding the Cauvery water dispute. Residents may see heightened police presence in major public squares like K.R. Puram today following the all-party meeting on this issue earlier this week.

### **Festival Fact-Check: Holi 2026**
*   **Date Correction:** Please note that **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 3rd and 4th, 2026.** There are no official Holi celebrations occurring today, August 6. 
*   **August Events:** While the "Festival of Colors" has passed, local venues like the **Garden City University** and **Grand Mercure (Gopalan Mall)** are hosting cultural sessions and student mixers this evening, but these are not Holi-related.

**Resident Advice:** Monitor the "Namma Ward" civic app for real-time water supply updates and use the Purple Line Metro to bypass the Tin Factory junction where possible.
