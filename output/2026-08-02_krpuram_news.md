**KR Puram Local Bulletin: August 2, 2026**

### **Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Lowry Under Bridge Closure:** Commuters are advised that the **KR Puram Lowry Under Bridge** remains closed for essential railway repair work. This closure is expected to continue through early August, causing significant diversions and heavy congestion on **Old Madras Road**.
*   **Hebbal Flyover Disruptions:** The **KR Puram up-ramp of the Hebbal Flyover** is currently closed to facilitate ongoing infrastructure upgrades. Traffic from KR Puram towards the city (Mekhri Circle) is being diverted; motorists should use the service road or alternative routes via Hennur.
*   **Metro Work Impact:** Construction for the **Namma Metro Blue Line** (ORR-Airport) and the **Pink Line** (Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere) continues to cause bottlenecks near Tin Factory. Trial runs for the Pink Line elevated section are now projected for late August or early September.

### **Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Water Supply Restrictions:** The **BWSSB** has enforced a strict ban on using potable water for non-essential purposes such as filling swimming pools, washing vehicles, or cleaning roads. Violators face a fine of **₹5,000**. Additionally, the deadline for installing mandatory water aerators (flow restrictors) passed on July 31; inspections are currently underway.
*   **Power Maintenance:** **BESCOM** has scheduled maintenance across several zones in East Bangalore this week to ensure grid stability during the monsoon. While no major blackout is reported for KR Puram today, intermittent outages may occur in nearby Whitefield and HSR Layout.
*   **Lalbagh Tunnel Road Protest:** A peaceful protest is being held today (August 2) at **7:30 AM near Lalbagh Rock** against the proposed tunnel road project. Residents traveling toward South Bangalore should expect local crowds and minor delays near the botanical garden.
