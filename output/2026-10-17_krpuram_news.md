### **KR Puram Local Bulletin: October 17, 2026**

#### **🚦 Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Tin Factory Junction:** Expect heavy congestion due to a recently added traffic signal that is causing significant backups. Commuters report increased travel times; many are opting for alternative routes through Belandur Lake or HAL to bypass this bottleneck.
*   **Hanging Bridge (Cable-Stayed Bridge):** Currently operational, but under increased scrutiny. Authorities have raised concerns over vegetation growth and potential structural deterioration of the 23-year-old bridge. No immediate closures are reported today, but slow-moving traffic is expected during peak hours.
*   **Advisory:** Use the **KR Puram Lowry Underbridge** for local connectivity unless heavy rains occur. During downpours, this underpass is a designated "monsoon hotspot" and should be avoided due to flash flooding risks.

#### **⚡ Civic Alerts & Utilities**
*   **Power Shutdown:** A scheduled maintenance outage is active today in parts of the **Mahadevapura/Whitefield zone** (bordering KR Puram) from **11:00 AM to 1:00 PM** for transformer replacement and feeder upgrades.
*   **Water Supply:** No major Cauvery water disruptions are reported for KR Puram today. However, BWSSB continues its city-wide crackdown on water misuse; residents are reminded that using drinking water for car washing or gardening may result in a ₹5,000 fine.
*   **Infrastructure:** The Bengaluru Central City Corporation (BCCC) has successfully cleared water stagnation issues at the KR Circle underpass, which may improve flow for those commuting toward the city center.
