### **KR Puram Local News Bulletin: October 11, 2026**

**Current Date:** Sunday, October 11, 2026
**Location:** KR Puram, Bangalore (Tin Factory, Hanging Bridge, Basava Nagar)

---

#### **1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Ongoing Choke Points:** Severe congestion continues at the **Tin Factory Junction** and the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge** due to the massive ₹378-crore Outer Ring Road (ORR) redevelopment project (Silk Board to KR Pura) and active Metro construction.
*   **Rain Impact:** Following heavy showers last week, residual waterlogging has been reported near the **KR Puram Metro Station** and the **Geddalahalli railway bridge** towards Hennur. Motorists are advised to use headlights due to overcast conditions and poor visibility.
*   **New Infrastructure:** The automated traffic signal at the mouth of the Hanging Bridge (Tin Factory side) is operational to streamline merging traffic from Mahadevapura and Kolar.

#### **2. Civic Alerts & Road Closures**
*   **Basava Nagar Main Road Closure:** A major closure began yesterday, **October 10**, and will last for **20 days** (until October 30). The road is shut for the construction of a stormwater drain bridge near Vibhutipura Lake.
    *   **Diversion:** Vehicles heading from Marathahalli/HAL towards Vignan Nagar/Kaggadasapura must use **Old Airport Main Road → Suranjandas Road → BEML Junction → Malleshpalya Main Road**.
*   **Infrastructure Work:** Service roads along the ORR are currently undergoing white-topping and drain upgrades, leading to narrowed lanes and slower movement.

#### **3. Festive Celebrations: Navratri vs. Holi 2026**
*   **Holi Update:** Please note that **Holi 2026** was celebrated on **March 3rd and 4th, 2026**. No Holi festivities are occurring today.
*   **Navratri 2026 Kickoff:** Today marks the official start of **Sharad Navratri (Pratipada)**. 
    *   **Local Event:** The **Whitefield Gujarati Association (WGA) Navratri 2026** begins today at the **KMM Royal Convention Centre** (near KR Puram/Hoskote Road) from 6:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Expect high footfall and localized traffic near the venue.
    *   **Durga Puja:** Preparations are underway at various pandals in KR Puram and Basavanagar, with main rituals scheduled to begin next week (October 15–16).
