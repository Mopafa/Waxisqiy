### **KR Puram Local Bulletin: October 3, 2026**

**1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Congestion Alert:** Expect heavy slow-moving traffic at the Tin Factory junction and Hanging Bridge. Recent structural assessments have raised concerns over the 23-year-old cable-stayed bridge due to vegetation growth and internal corrosion, leading to stricter lane monitoring.
*   **Automated Signals:** The automated signal at the mouth of the bridge (near the HP fuel station) is operational. Commuters from Mahadevapura and Hoskote should factor in an additional **15–20 minutes** for transit.
*   **Alternative Routes:** If traveling toward Hebbal, consider the route via **Kasturi Nagar** or the **Banaswadi** inner roads to bypass the main junction.

**2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Metro Expansion:** The 17-km Metro extension from **KR Pura to Hoskote** has officially moved to the Detailed Project Report (DPR) stage. Initial site surveys may cause minor curb-side disruptions near the NH-75 stretch.
*   **Land Acquisition:** The BDA has issued final notifications for the **Peripheral Ring Road (PRR-1)**. Residents in affected villages around KR Puram and Bidarahalli are advised to check survey numbers before property transactions.
*   **Illegal PG Crackdown:** Following recent High Court guidelines, civic authorities continue to seal illegal PG accommodations in KR Puram. Ensure your residence has a valid trade license to avoid sudden utility disconnections.
*   **Event Alert:** The **Hiriyara Jaatre 2026** (Annual Elders' Fest) is taking place today at New Horizon Gurukul, Kadubeesanahalli (nearby), which may increase local vehicle density until 5:00 PM.

**3. Festival Watch: Holi 2026 vs. Current Events**
*   **Holi Status:** Please note that **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 3–4, 2026**. There are no Holi celebrations scheduled for today.
*   **Dasara Season:** Today marks the official inauguration of the **Mysuru Dasara (Oct 3–12, 2026)**. While the main festivities are in Mysuru, local temple celebrations and "Ayudha Puja" preparations are beginning in KR Puram and across Bangalore. 
*   **Cultural Tonight:** If you are looking for entertainment today, the **"Kerala Rocks India Tour"** (Malayalam/Alternative Rock) kicks off at DND The Venue in HSR Layout at 7:00 PM.

**Quick Tip for Residents:** Check live maps before heading toward the railway station; the foot-over-bridge (FOB) connecting the KR Puram Metro to the Railway Station is fully open and is the fastest way to transition between modes.
