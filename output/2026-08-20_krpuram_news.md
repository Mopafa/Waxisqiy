**KR Puram Local News Bulletin: Thursday, August 20, 2026**

### **Traffic Advisory: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Railway Bridge Repairs:** Commuters should expect severe delays at the **Bennaganahalli – KR Puram stretch** of Old Madras Road. Repairs on the railway overbridge are ongoing and will continue through late August.
*   **Recommended Diversion:** Traffic police advise motorists traveling towards KR Puram to take a **left turn at Kasturinagar** toward the Baiyappanahalli metro station and use the service road of the Outer Ring Road (ORR) to reach the Tin Factory junction.
*   **Ongoing Congestion:** Heavy traffic is reported at the mouth of the **Hanging Bridge** (Tin Factory side) due to both the bridge signal and the ongoing Metro Blue Line construction. Commuters are advised to factor in an additional 20–30 minutes for this stretch.

### **Local Civic Alerts & Safety**
*   **Footpath Clearance Drive:** Greater Bengaluru Development Minister Krishna Byre Gowda has ordered the **Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA)** to expedite the removal of footpath encroachments and abandoned vehicles in the KR Puram and Hebbal sectors. Repeat encroachers will face double penalties starting this week.
*   **Food Safety Raid:** A surprise inspection at **KR Puram Railway Station eateries** today revealed unhygienic conditions and the use of suspected illegal food coloring. Residents are advised to exercise caution at station-side food stalls.
*   **Cyber-Security Alert:** A resident in Jayanthi Nagar, KR Puram, recently lost ₹9.96 lakh to a **KYC update scam**. Local police remind residents never to download APK files sent via WhatsApp or share banking OTPs with unknown callers claiming to be from nationalized banks.
*   **ORR White-topping Delay:** Road works on the Silk Board-KR Puram stretch remain a point of contention. The ORR Companies Association (ORRCA) has officially requested a pause on major white-topping until the Namma Metro Blue Line is operational to avoid complete gridlock.

### **Holi 2026 Celebrations**
*   **Official Date:** Please note that the primary **Holi 2026 festival occurred on March 3rd and 4th**. 
*   **Today's Schedule:** There are **no major public Holi events or rain dances** scheduled in the KR Puram or Gopalan Mall area today, August 20. Most local festivities currently center around the upcoming **Sri Krishna Janmashtami** (September 4). 

**Resident Tip:** With the "Freedom from Waste" campaign active, residents can report uncleared debris or abandoned vehicles directly to GBA zonal officials to assist in the August cleanliness drive.
