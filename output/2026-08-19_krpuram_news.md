Here is your local news bulletin for **KR Puram, Bangalore**, for **Wednesday, August 19, 2026**.

### **1. Traffic & Infrastructure Update**
*   **Yeshwantpur–KR Puram Tolled Flyover:** Major news today as **B-SMILE** officially invited tenders for a new **28-km elevated corridor** connecting Yeshwantpur to the Tin Factory. This ₹3,536-crore project will be the city’s first tolled flyover, designed to bypass 11 major congestion points.
*   **ORR Congestion Alert:** The **Outer Ring Road Companies’ Association (ORRCA)** has formally objected to a ₹450-crore "white-topping" project scheduled for the stretch between Central Silk Board and KR Puram. Tech firms are calling for the work to be halted until the **Metro Blue Line** is operational to prevent catastrophic gridlock.
*   **Tin Factory / Hanging Bridge:** Expect heavy slow-moving traffic throughout the evening rush hour. Ongoing Metro station work near the KR Puram bridge continues to cause lane narrowings. Commuters are advised to use the service roads near Kasturinagar to avoid the main Tin Factory bottleneck.
