**Local News Bulletin: KR Puram, Bengaluru**
**Date:** Sunday, September 20, 2026
**Status:** Live Updates for Residents

### **1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Current Condition:** Expect heavy congestion at the **Tin Factory junction** and the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge** (Cable Bridge). This remains one of the city's primary bottlenecks due to the merging of traffic from Old Madras Road, Outer Ring Road, and Whitefield.
*   **Structural Note:** Recent structural assessments (late August 2026) have highlighted concerns regarding vegetation growth and concrete cracking on the 23-year-old KR Puram cable-stayed bridge. While the bridge remains open, commuters are advised to maintain lane discipline to avoid further strain on the structure.
*   **Police Alert:** Bengaluru Traffic Police (BTP) continue their awareness campaign against wrong-way driving. Vehicles are restricted on certain flyovers to streamline peak-hour flow. Use the **ASTraM app** for real-time alternate route suggestions.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts (BESCOM & BWSSB)**
*   **Power Outages:** BESCOM and KPTCL are currently in the middle of a scheduled maintenance window (September 15–29, 2026). Multiple areas in the **East Zone** and **Mahadevapura division** (bordering KR Puram) are experiencing periodic outages between **9:00 AM and 5:00 PM**.
*   **Water Supply:** The major 60-hour Cauvery water cut that started on September 15 has concluded. However, residents in some KR Puram pockets may still experience low pressure as the system fully re-pressurizes. Ensure overhead tanks are filled.
*   **Emergency Contact:** For electricity issues, dial **1912**. For water-related grievances, contact the **BWSSB helpline** at **1916**.
