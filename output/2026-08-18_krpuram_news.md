**KR Puram Local News Bulletin – Tuesday, August 18, 2026**

### 🚦 Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge
*   **Major Bottleneck:** Commuters at the **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge** junction should expect heavy congestion throughout the day due to ongoing construction on the **Metro Blue Line (Phase 2A)** and road revamp works on the 17-km stretch toward Silk Board.

*   **Recommended Route:** Use the **K.R. Puram Cable-Stayed Bridge** to bypass the surface-level Tin Factory junction if traveling toward Ramamurthy Nagar or ITI Colony.
