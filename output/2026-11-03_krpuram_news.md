### **1. Traffic & Transit: Tin Factory / Hanging Bridge**
*   **Metro Construction Update:** Traffic around the Tin Factory and Hanging Bridge continues to be impacted by ongoing work on the **Namma Metro Blue Line** (KR Puram to Central Silk Board). *   **Infrastructure Alert:** Recent structural assessments of the 23-year-old Hanging Bridge have highlighted concerns over vegetation growth and moisture ingress. While the bridge remains operational, light vehicle users are advised to stay in marked lanes to avoid uneven surfaces.

*   **Public Safety:** Local police have issued a fresh advisory regarding **mobile phone theft and UPI scams** on public buses following a pickpocketing incident today involving an elderly commuter. Secure your devices and avoid sharing PINs while in transit.
