**KR Puram Local Bulletin: August 27, 2026**

### **1. Traffic & Transit Updates**
*   **Metro Alert (Purple Line):** Commuters should expect significant disruptions today. Metro rail services between **Whitefield (Kadugodi) and Krishnarajapura (KR Puram)** are suspended from **7:00 AM to 1:00 PM** for essential safety tests. During this window, services on the Purple Line will only be available between Swami Vivekananda Road and Mysore Road.
*   **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge:** Heavy congestion is reported at the Tin Factory junction. Lingering traffic from yesterday’s Eid Milad processions and ongoing work on the Yeshwanthpur–KR Puram elevated corridor tender are compounding delays.
*   **Alternative Routes:** For those traveling toward Hebbal or Indiranagar, traffic police suggest taking the service road of the Outer Ring Road or diverting at Kasturinagar to bypass the Hanging Bridge bottleneck.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Power Outages (BESCOM):** Scheduled maintenance is expected to affect parts of the **KR Puram, Mahadevapura, and Hoodi** belts today. Maintenance windows typically range from **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM**. Residents are advised to check the *BESCOM Mithra* app for real-time restoration status.
*   **Water Supply (BWSSB):** While no major shutdown is scheduled for today, a significant 15-hour disruption is planned for **tomorrow (Friday, August 28)** for Cauvery Stage IV maintenance. Residents should store adequate water today. Note that the city-wide ban on using drinking water for car washing, swimming pools, and construction remains in effect.
*   **Civic Action:** Starting today, the **Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA)** will begin cleaning identified vacant plots that owners failed to maintain. The cleaning costs will be billed to the owners through property tax.

### **3. Holi 2026 Celebrations**
*   **Correction:** There are **no Holi celebrations occurring today**. In 2026, the festival of Holi was celebrated on **March 3rd and 4th**. Any events listed for today are likely unrelated to the festival of colors.
*   **Today's Events:** Residents can find local entertainment at the **Venkateshwara 4K Cinema** in KR Puram, which is screening *Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-ups* (Kannada) and *Irumudi* (Telugu) today.

**Resident Advice:** Charge all electronic devices before 10:00 AM and plan your commute around the mid-day Metro closure to avoid being stranded.
