**Local Bulletin: KR Puram, Bengaluru**
**Date:** Thursday, October 15, 2026
**Time:** 2:00 PM IST

### **1. Traffic & Weather Alert: Tin Factory / Hanging Bridge**
*   **Heavy Rain Impact:** A massive thunderstorm has moved across East Bengaluru today. Commuters are reporting significant **waterlogging** around the KR Puram Metro Station and the Tin Factory junction.
*   **Slow Movement:** Traffic at the Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge stretch is currently moving at a crawl due to poor visibility and water accumulation on the carriageway. Motorists are advised to use the cable-stayed bridge instead of the underpasses, which are prone to flooding.
*   **Safety Warning:** Authorities have recently flagged structural concerns (concrete cracking and vegetation growth) on the 23-year-old KR Puram Hanging Bridge. Residents are urged to drive cautiously, especially under heavy load and rain conditions.

### **2. Civic Alerts & Public Notices**
*   **PG Accommodation Crackdown:** The Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) has intensified inspections in KR Puram. Over 2,500 PG facilities in the East Zone were recently found operating without valid licenses or fire-safety clearances; residents are advised to verify the legal status of their accommodations.
*   **Power & Water:** 
    *   **BESCOM:** No major scheduled power cuts are listed for KR Puram today, though intermittent outages may occur due to rain-related feeder faults.
    *   **BWSSB:** Following a new notification, KR Puram is now fully integrated into the expanded BWSSB jurisdiction. However, low-lying areas are currently on alert for storm-water drain overflow risk due to the ongoing monsoon intensity.
*   **Helpline:** Residents can report rain-related emergencies or civic issues via the **BTP ASTraM app** or by calling **1912** for BESCOM.
