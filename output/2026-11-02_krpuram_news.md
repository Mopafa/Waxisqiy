**KR PURAM LOCAL BULLETIN: NOVEMBER 2, 2026**

### **1. Traffic & Transit Updates**
*   **Tin Factory/Hanging Bridge:** Heavy congestion persists at the Tin Factory junction due to ongoing **Blue Line Metro** trial runs and construction at the KR Puram–Central Silk Board stretch. Motorists are advised to expect significant delays at the bridge bottleneck near the KR Puram Railway Station.
*   **Hebbal Flyover Diversion:** If traveling towards Mehkri Circle from KR Puram, the **right-turn ban** under the Hebbal flyover remains in effect. Use alternate routes via Lingarajpuram or Nagawara-Tannery Road to enter the city.
*   **Bus Fare Hike:** The Karnataka government has officially announced a **bus fare hike starting November 5, 2026**. BMTC fares are expected to increase by up to 45%, following recent hikes in milk and curd prices.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Power:** **BESCOM** has confirmed there are **no scheduled power cuts** for KR Puram or other major Bengaluru zones today, November 2. 
*   **Water:** No emergency water disruptions are reported for KR Puram today. However, residents are encouraged to maintain storage as BWSSB continues periodic maintenance on Cauvery Stage V pipelines across the city.
*   **Waste Management:** BBMP has intensified the "Ward One" campaign; ensure dry and wet waste is segregated for morning collection to avoid penalties.

### **3. Festival Watch: Holi vs. Diwali 2026**
*   **Holi 2026:** Please note that **Holi was celebrated on March 3–4, 2026**. There are no Holi celebrations today.
*   **Diwali 2026:** Today marks the beginning of the **Diwali festive week**. The main celebration (Lakshmi Puja) is scheduled for **Sunday, November 8, 2026**. 
*   **Civic Notice:** The state has announced a **2-hour window (8:00 PM to 10:00 PM)** for bursting green crackers during the upcoming Deepavali celebrations to control pollution levels.

**Resident Tip:** With Metro work reducing road width near the Hanging Bridge, consider using the **Purple Line Metro** from KR Puram station for travel towards Majestic or Whitefield to avoid the Tin Factory gridlock.
