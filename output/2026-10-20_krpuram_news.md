**KR PURAM LOCAL BULLETIN: OCTOBER 20, 2026**

### **1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Festive Congestion:** Expect heavy traffic today and tomorrow (Oct 20–21) due to **Durga Devi immersion processions**. Traffic police have issued alerts for the KR Puram and Nelamangala corridors.
*   **Key Bottlenecks:** The **Tin Factory Junction** and **Hanging Bridge** remain slow-moving zones due to the convergence of festive crowds and ongoing **Metro construction** on the Outer Ring Road (ORR) stretch.
*   **Advisory:** Motorists traveling towards Whitefield or ITPL are advised to use the **KR Puram Cable-Stayed Bridge** or Hoodi routes to bypass the heavy pile-up at the Tin Factory underpass.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts & Weather**
*   **Rain Alert:** The IMD has issued a **Yellow Alert** for Bengaluru, with heavy showers expected to continue until **October 24**. 
*   **Waterlogging Risk:** BBMP has identified the KR Puram area as a "monsoon hotspot." Residents are urged to **avoid waterlogging-prone underpasses** (such as the ITI Depo underpass) during sudden downpours.
*   **Utility Works:** While no major power shutdowns are scheduled specifically for KR Puram today, nearby Whitefield has a transformer replacement scheduled (11:00 AM – 1:00 PM), which may cause minor voltage fluctuations in adjacent grid lines.

### **3. Festival Celebrations: Dussehra vs. Holi**
*   **Clarification:** Please note that **Holi 2026** was celebrated on **March 3rd and 4th**. 
*   **Today’s Events:** Today, **October 20**, is the major celebration of **Dussehra (Vijayadashami)**. 
    *   **Durga Puja:** The **East Bengaluru Cultural Association (EBCA)** and local pandals in KR Puram are hosting rituals like *Sindoor Daan* and *Sandhi Puja*.
    *   **Immersion:** Massive immersion processions are expected at local lakes (like Halasuru and Seegehalli) starting this evening.
    *   **Ravan Dahan:** Local community grounds in KR Puram and Mahadevapura will host symbolic effigy burning events after sunset (approx. 6:30 PM).

**Resident Safety Tip:** If you are heading out for celebrations, use public transport (Namma Metro Purple Line) where possible to avoid the gridlock around the Hanging Bridge.
