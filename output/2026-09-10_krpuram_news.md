**KR PURAM LOCAL BULLETIN: SEPTEMBER 10, 2026**

### **🚦 Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Infrastructure Proposals:** As of today, the Bengaluru Traffic Police have officially submitted a proposal to the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) for a new grade-separated flyover/underpass at the **KR Puram Railway Station** and **Bhattarahalli Junction** to address chronic congestion.
*   **Ongoing Restrictions:** Commuters should note that the phased traffic restrictions on major city flyovers, which began on September 4, remain in effect. While the KR Puram Hanging Bridge is currently open to all vehicles, traffic police are strictly monitoring the recently shifted bus stop near the Tin Factory skywalk to prevent "criss-cross" congestion.
*   **Peak Hour Advisory:** Expect heavy slow-downs between 9:00 AM – 11:30 AM and 5:30 PM – 8:30 PM. The automated signal at the mouth of the Hanging Bridge is being manually overridden by wardens today to manage the high volume of inbound Kolar/Hosakote traffic.

### **⚡ Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Power Shutdown:** BESCOM has scheduled maintenance work today affecting several feeders in the East Zone. Residents in **Ramamurthy Nagar**, **Kasturinagar**, and parts of **B Channasandra** may experience intermittent power outages between **10:00 AM and 5:00 PM**.
*   **Water Supply Warning:** While supply is normal today, **BWSSB** has issued a major alert for next week. A **60-hour water shutdown** is scheduled from **September 15 to 17, 2026**, for Cauvery Stage V maintenance. Residents are advised to clean storage tanks and begin conserving water starting now.
*   **Property Civic Note:** BBMP (now under GBA) has issued a reminder regarding "Betterment Charges" for property owners in newly developed layouts around KR Puram; unpaid charges may stall Khata transfers.

### **🎉 Celebrations & Holi 2026**
*   **Holi Update:** Please note that **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 3–4, 2026**. There are no Holi festivities occurring today, as it is a spring festival.
*   **Current Festivities:** The primary focus for local residents today is the preparation for **Ganesh Chaturthi**, which begins this coming Monday, **September 14, 2026**.
*   **KR Puram Markets:** Expect high footfall and local traffic around the KR Puram Market area today and through the weekend as residents shop for festival essentials. Local pandals are currently being erected near the Hanging Bridge and Devasandra Main Road.

**Resident Tip:** Check the **BESCOM Mithra app** for real-time restoration times if your power is affected by the maintenance work today.
