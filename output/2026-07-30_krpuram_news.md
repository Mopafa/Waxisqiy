**KR PURAM LOCAL BULLETIN: July 30, 2026**

### **🚦 Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Hanging Bridge (Suspension Bridge):** High congestion is reported at the base of the bridge on the Byappanahalli side. Cross-directional movement between the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and Old Madras Road (OMR) is causing significant bottlenecks. 
*   **Tin Factory Junction:** Expect heavy slow-moving traffic due to unauthorized bus stops at the start of the flyover and ongoing Metro construction activities. 
*   **Weather Impact:** Due to the **Orange Alert** for heavy rainfall (see Civic Alerts), travel times are expected to increase by 30–40 minutes during peak hours. Avoid the KR Puram railway station bottleneck where waterlogging typically slows down movement.

### **📢 Civic Alerts**
*   **Weather Warning:** The IMD has issued an **Orange Alert** for Bengaluru Urban today, July 30. Expect heavy showers and gusty winds (40-50 kmph). 
*   **Flood Risk:** Residents are advised to avoid the **KR Puram Lowry Underbridge** and surrounding low-lying areas during heavy downpours, as these have been flagged as high-risk monsoon hotspots for waterlogging.
*   **Water Supply:** While no specific shutdown is scheduled for KR Puram today, the Cauvery Water Management Authority (CWMA) is meeting today to discuss water releases; stay tuned for potential updates on local supply schedules.
