**KR Puram Local Bulletin – Wednesday, September 30, 2026**

### **🚦 Traffic & Road Safety**
*   **Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge:** Expect heavy congestion due to rain-induced waterlogging. The **KR Puram cable-stayed bridge** remains under close structural monitoring; structural concerns (cracks/corrosion) reported last month may lead to periodic lane restrictions or slow-moving traffic.
*   **Accident Alert:** A fatal accident occurred today on **Kurudusonnenahalli Main Road** (under KR Puram Traffic Police limits). A 12-year-old boy tragically lost his life after a two-wheeler fall. Motorists are advised to expect localized delays and a heightened police presence in the Kurudusonnenahalli/Hoskote road vicinity for investigations.
*   **Alternative Routes:** Commuters from TC Palya and Ramamurthy Nagar are urged to avoid wrong-side driving near the railway underpass, as traffic police are conducting a crackdown to prevent the "daily chaos" seen recently.

### **📢 Civic Alerts**
*   **Weather Warning:** The IMD has issued a **Yellow Alert** for Bengaluru today. Expect moderate to heavy rain with thunderstorms this late afternoon and evening. 
*   **BBMP Advisory:** Residents in low-lying areas, particularly **Sai Layout, Pai Layout, and Horamavu**, should be on alert for potential flooding as stormwater drains remain at high capacity from recent spells.
*   **New Infrastructure Plan:** The Karnataka government today notified a **"New Road Grid"** proposal. This long-term plan targets widening narrow roads in areas surrounding KR Puram to widths of up to 45–90 meters over the next 5–10 years to address chronic infrastructure deficits.
