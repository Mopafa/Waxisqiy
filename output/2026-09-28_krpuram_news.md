**KR Puram Local Bulletin: September 28, 2026**

**Traffic & Infrastructure**
*   **Hanging Bridge / Cable-Stayed Bridge:** The 23-year-old KR Puram cable-stayed bridge remains under structural watch. Recent assessments have flagged concerns over concrete cracking and reinforcement corrosion; commuters are advised to expect periodic inspections and minor delays as authorities weigh long-term repairs.
*   **Tin Factory Junction:** Traffic is currently heavy on the KR Puram-to-Hoskote stretch. While no major accidents were reported this morning, long-term congestion persists as work continues to link the city to the new Bengaluru-Chennai Expressway (BCE).
*   **Upcoming Toll Corridor:** Plans for a 28-km Yeshwanthpur-KR Puram flyover are active, with officials projecting it as a major traffic decongestant once construction phases begin in the region.

**Civic Alerts**
*   **Illegal PG Sealing Drive:** The Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) has announced a major crackdown starting today. Authorities are moving to seal illegal Paying Guest (PG) accommodations that lack proper licenses or fire safety clearances. Power and water connections to these buildings will be disconnected.
*   **Power Outages:** While no specific feeder-level shutdown is scheduled for KR Puram today, maintenance work in the adjacent Hebbal division (running through September 29) may cause tangential outages or voltage fluctuations in neighboring areas.
*   **Water Supply:** No emergency water cuts were issued for today. However, residents are advised to monitor the status of local lines following the city-wide "illegal PG" disconnection drive which may impact shared utility lines in mixed-use residential blocks.

**Festival Clarification: Holi 2026**
*   **Date Correction:** Please note that **Holi 2026 was celebrated on March 3rd and 4th, 2026**. There are no scheduled Holi celebrations today. The city is currently in a transition period following the Ganesha Utsava festivities (which concluded on Sept 26).

**Local News**
*   **Triple Murder Case Update:** The KR Puram police recently filed a massive 2,085-page chargesheet in the Seegehalli triple murder case against a tech professional and her partner. This continues to be a major talking point in the local community as the case moves to trial.
