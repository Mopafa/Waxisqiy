**KR PURAM LOCAL NEWS BULLETIN: MONDAY, AUGUST 10, 2026**

For residents of KR Puram and surrounding areas, here is your concise update on traffic, civic alerts, and festivals for today.

### **1. Traffic Alert: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Safety Incident:** A major traffic disruption was reported this morning after a **metal sheet fell from a Metro construction bridge** onto the road near the KR Puram stretch. While no casualties were reported, the incident has caused significant rubbernecking and lane closures for safety inspections.
*   **Metro Construction:** Congestion remains high at the Tin Factory junction due to ongoing work on the **Blue Line (Airport Metro)**. BMRCL has confirmed the Silk Board–KR Puram stretch is currently on track for a **late 2026 opening**, but commuters should expect an additional 15–20 minutes of travel time during peak hours.
*   **Alternate Routes:** To avoid the Hanging Bridge bottleneck, commuters heading toward Baiyappanahalli are advised to take a left at Kasturinagar and use the service roads.

### **2. Civic Alerts & Local News**
*   **Interstate Travel Alert:** Pro-Kannada activists led by Vatal Nagaraj have initiated a **blockade at the Attibele border** starting at 11:00 AM today (Aug 10). While not a full city bandh, interstate travel to/from Tamil Nadu via Hosur Road is heavily restricted. This is a precursor to a **statewide bandh scheduled for August 13**.
*   **Civic Irregularities:** Three government surveyors at the **KR Puram ADLR office** were booked recently following an internal inquiry into irregularities involving over 100 land survey applications. 
*   **Flooding Preparedness:** Following recent rains, BBMP remains on alert in **Sai Layout and Pai Layout**. Officials have identified a railway bridge over a stormwater drain as a primary cause of local flooding and are coordinating repairs to prevent further waterlogging.
