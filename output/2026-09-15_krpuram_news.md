### **KR Puram Local News Bulletin: September 15, 2026**

#### **1. Traffic Alert: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **Current Status:** Heavy congestion is reported at the Tin Factory junction and the KR Puram Hanging Bridge. Commuters are experiencing average delays of **15–20 minutes** for every 10 km during peak hours.
*   **Bottlenecks:** The stretch towards Hebbal remains a significant bottleneck due to haphazard BMTC bus stops near the beginning of the flyover and the railway station underpass. 
*   **Advice:** If traveling toward Whitefield or ITPL, consider using the KR Puram Cable-Stayed Bridge as an alternative to the lower service roads, which are heavily packed due to festive movement.

#### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Water Supply Disruption:** The BWSSB has issued a major alert for a **60-hour water shutdown** starting today, September 15, and lasting until 1:00 PM on September 17. This is due to emergency maintenance at the Cauvery Stage V pumping station. Residents in KR Puram and surrounding areas are advised to store sufficient water.
*   **Garbage Collection Alert:** Waste management services in KR Puram may be delayed today. Garbage collection workers (auto drivers and loaders) have launched an indefinite protest at Freedom Park starting today, demanding permanent employment status.
*   **Civic Enforcement:** BBMP has intensified "spot fines" for open dumping of garbage. Ensure all household waste is handed over to designated collection vehicles to avoid penalties.

#### **3. Festival Update: Ganesh Chaturthi & Holi 2026**
*   **Ganesh Chaturthi:** While Holi 2026 was celebrated in March, today marks the second day of the **Ganesh Chaturthi** festival (which began on September 14). Local pandals across KR Puram and Devasandra are hosting community prayers.
*   **Rishi Panchami:** Today (September 15) also marks **Rishi Panchami**, a significant day for traditional rituals following Ganesh Chaturthi.
*   **Safety Note:** Expect increased pedestrian activity and temporary slow-moving traffic near local temples and pandals throughout the evening.

***

**Resident Advisory:** Plan your commute early to navigate the combined impact of the water maintenance project and festive traffic. Check for updates on the BTP (Bengaluru Traffic Police) social media handles for real-time diversions.
