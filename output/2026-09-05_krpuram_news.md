### **KR Puram Local Bulletin: Saturday, September 5, 2026**

**1. Traffic Update: Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge**
*   **New Flyover Restrictions:** As of yesterday (Sept 4), the Bengaluru Traffic Police have implemented trial restrictions on five major elevated corridors. While the **KR Puram Hanging Bridge** is not under a total ban, expect spillover congestion as two-wheelers and autos are now barred 24/7 from the International Airport Road Elevated Corridor and Peenya Flyover.
*   **Structural Concerns:** Residents are advised to exercise caution on the **Hanging Bridge**; recent structural assessments have flagged concerns regarding vegetation growth and concrete cracking on the 23-year-old structure.
*   **Tin Factory Junction:** Routine heavy congestion is reported at the Tin Factory bottleneck. Metro construction for the Blue Line (Phase-2B) continues to impact lane availability towards the airport.

**2. Civic Alerts: Power & Water**
*   **BESCOM Power Cuts:** A series of scheduled maintenance outages begins **today, September 5**, and will continue through September 15. Power interruptions are expected between **10:00 AM and 5:00 PM** in various pockets of the Mahadevapura zone. Nearby areas such as NRI Layout and HRBR Layout may experience longer disruptions.
*   **Water Supply:** No major BWSSB shutdowns are scheduled for today. However, be prepared for a **60-hour city-wide water disruption** later this month (September 15–17) due to Cauvery Stage V maintenance. 

**3. Today’s Celebrations & Events**
*   **Holi 2026 Clarification:** Please note that **Holi was celebrated on March 4, 2026**. There are no official Holi festivities occurring today.
*   **Teacher’s Day:** Today is September 5, observed across India. Local schools in KR Puram, including NPS and IZee Group, are hosting student-led cultural programs to honor educators. 
*   **Vyasa Puja (ISKCON Bangalore):** Today marks the appearance day of Srila Prabhupada. ISKCON Bangalore is hosting a grand **Vyasa Puja** celebration. Events include a formal *abhisheka* and homages, with live streaming of the main rituals starting at **6:30 PM**.

**Resident Advisory:** Plan for slightly longer commutes due to the new city-wide flyover rules and check your local BESCOM group for specific block-level power schedules.
