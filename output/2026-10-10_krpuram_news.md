**KR Puram Resident Bulletin: Saturday, October 10, 2026**

### **1. Traffic & Transit Updates**
*   **Tin Factory & Hanging Bridge:** Expect heavy congestion throughout the day. While no major accidents are reported this morning, Saturday peak volumes combined with ongoing metro-related work near the Tin Factory junction are causing "stop-and-go" traffic. Commuters heading toward **Whitefield** or **Hebbal** should account for an additional 20–30 minutes of travel time.

*   **Metro Alert:** Experts continue to monitor the structural health of the 23-year-old KR Puram cable-stayed bridge. While open to traffic, residents are advised to stick to designated lanes as maintenance teams may conduct periodic inspections.

### **2. Local Civic Alerts**
*   **Weather (Yellow Alert):** The IMD has issued a **Yellow Alert** for Bengaluru today. Heavy rainfall and thunderstorms are expected in the late afternoon and evening. Residents in low-lying areas near KR Puram lake and the railway underpasses should remain vigilant for waterlogging.
*   **Protest Activity:** A city-wide protest by the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) is scheduled at **Freedom Park** today (October 10). While not in KR Puram, this may affect the movement of police personnel and cause secondary traffic delays on major arterial roads leading to the city center.
*   **Community Cleanup:** Following successful debris clearance on **Wind Tunnel Road** earlier this week, the BSWML (Greater Bengaluru Authority) has announced continued beautification efforts over the weekend. Expect minor sidewalk obstructions in neighboring wards.
